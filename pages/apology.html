A LETTER FROM MY HEART TO YOURS

My Baby Julie,

Before anything else, I want to say sorry. I’m sorry for the times I failed to understand you, for my mistakes, and for the moments when my words or actions may have hurt you. I’m also sorry if I made you feel uncomfortable or overwhelmed.

I know I am not perfect. There are things I still need to learn about listening, giving you space, and saying what I feel with care. I’m willing to hear where I went wrong and make real changes, even when those conversations are difficult.

I never want you to feel that you have to carry the weight of my emotions alone. My feelings are mine to express responsibly. I want to become someone who understands you better and loves you in ways that make you feel safe and appreciated.

I don’t want my pride to become
bigger than my love for you.

I’m not writing this because I expect everything to be forgotten immediately. An apology needs patience and actions behind it. I’m writing this because you matter to me, and our relationship matters to me.

You can take your time with these words. I want to listen to your side, too.

With an open heart,
James ♡
