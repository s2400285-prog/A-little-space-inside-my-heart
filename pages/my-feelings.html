02 / A PAGE I’VE BEEN AFRAID TO WRITE

Something has been
hurting me.

Not to point a finger. Just to let you into my heart.

A little honesty…

Baby, I want to tell you something that I have been keeping inside. These past few days, I have been hurt by some things that happened between us. There are moments when I feel ignored, and honestly, it makes me sad.

Sometimes I just want to receive a message from you without having to feel like I’m always waiting. Sometimes I want to feel that you also want to talk to me, that you remember me, and that you still look forward to spending time with me.

02 — my feelings
…with a lot of love.

I don’t need your attention every second of the day. I know you have your own responsibilities and personal space, and I respect that. But I hope you understand that I also need reassurance and effort.

I don’t want to feel like I’m asking for too much just because I want to feel important in your life.

I am not saying this to blame you. I am saying this because I want you to understand my heart, too.

03 — room for yours, too
Both of our feelings can matter at the same time.

I know a quiet moment can mean many things. I’d like us to talk about a rhythm that feels comfortable for both of us, without expecting either of us to always be available.
