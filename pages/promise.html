05 / SOMETHING I WANT TO KEEP CHOOSING

A promise
from my heart.

To listen with care. To speak with kindness. To keep learning.

FOR YOU, FOR ME, FOR US

Baby Julie,

I don’t want us to become distant just because we have difficult days. I want us to be able to express what hurts us without being afraid of how the other person might react.

I am sorry for my mistakes. I hope you can understand what I’ve been feeling, too, and I want to give your feelings the same care.

I don’t want to be the only one trying to fix things, and I don’t want you to feel that way either. I want us to meet halfway. I want us both to make an effort, listen to each other, and remember that we are two people with feelings who deserve to be understood.

I still want the little conversations, random updates, silly moments, unexpected smiles, and simple good mornings.

I don’t need a perfect relationship, Baby.
I just want us both to feel loved.

I want something where we both feel valued, respected, and never alone in trying. I can start with my own part: listening without interrupting, asking instead of assuming, and giving us time to pause when a conversation feels overwhelming.

I love you, Julie. Even when things are difficult, I still want to choose understanding over pride and communication over silence.

Always remember that your feelings matter to me, but I hope you remember that mine matter, too.

With love,
James ♡
